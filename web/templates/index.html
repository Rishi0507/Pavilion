How it works
  1. A score is set. One target, the same for everybody, chosen by simulating the day thousands of times until it makes a contest.
  2. You bowl first. Five bowlers, four overs each. Every over you choose who bowls. Stop them getting there.
  3. Then you bat. Same score, other side. Six attacking overs to spend across twenty. Get there yourself.

The catch is that your best bowler only has four overs, so using him early means someone worse bowls at the end. You are scored on your choices rather than your luck: every decision is compared against what else was available, before the ball was bowled.
